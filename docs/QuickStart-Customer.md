# Car Parts App - Quick Start Guide for Customers

## Welcome! 👋

This guide will help you browse and order car parts in 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Login

1. Go to the Car Parts App
2. Enter your **phone number** and **password**
3. Click **Login**

**Forgot your password?** Contact your store manager.

---

## Step 2: Browse the Shop

Once logged in, you'll see the **SHOP** tab.

- **Search**: Type a part name or number in the search bar
- **Filter by Category**: Click the category buttons to narrow down
- **View Details**: Click a part to see price, availability, and images

---

## Step 3: Check Availability

Each part shows:
- **Price** (in your currency)
- **Stock status** (In Stock / Limited / Out of Stock)
- **Description** and **photos**

---

## Step 4: Add to Cart

1. Click a part
2. Click **Add to Cart**
3. Confirm the quantity
4. Click **Add**

---

## Step 5: Checkout

1. Click **Cart** (top right)
2. Review your items
3. Click **Order**
4. Enter your delivery address
5. Click **Place Order**

**Your order is now submitted!** You'll receive updates via email/WhatsApp.

---

## Need Help?

- **Order Status**: Click **Orders** tab to see your orders
- **Contact Support**: Click the **Help** button (bottom right)
- **Call Store**: Ask staff for the phone number

---

## Quick Tips

✅ **Keep your password safe** — don't share it  
✅ **Check stock before ordering** — some parts may take time  
✅ **Save your delivery address** for faster checkout next time  
✅ **Track your order** in the Orders tab
