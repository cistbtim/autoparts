# Car Parts App - Quick Start Guide for Managers

## Welcome! 👋

This guide covers the main features you'll use daily: inventory, orders, invoicing, and reports.

---

## Step 1: Login & Dashboard

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Click **Login**
3. You'll see the **Dashboard** with key metrics

**Dashboard shows:**
- Total orders this week
- Stock status overview
- Pending invoices
- Recent activity

---

## Step 2: Manage Inventory

Click the **INVENTORY** tab.

### View Stock
- See all parts and their quantities
- Search by name or part number
- Filter by category

### Add a New Part
1. Click **+ Add Part**
2. Enter part details (name, number, category, price, cost)
3. Upload an image (optional)
4. Click **Save**

### Update Stock
1. Find the part
2. Click **Edit**
3. Change quantity or price
4. Click **Save**

### Move Stock
1. Click **Stock Moves**
2. Click **+ New Move**
3. Select from/to location
4. Enter quantity
5. Click **Complete**

---

## Step 3: Process Orders

Click the **ORDERS** tab.

### View Orders
- **New Orders**: Orders waiting to be confirmed
- **In Progress**: Being packed/shipped
- **Completed**: Delivered

### Pack an Order
1. Click on the order
2. Review items
3. Click **Mark as Packed**
4. Print the packing slip (optional)

### Ship an Order
1. Click **Ready to Ship**
2. Enter tracking number (optional)
3. Click **Mark as Shipped**
4. Customer gets notified

---

## Step 4: Create Invoices

Click **INVOICING** tab.

### Generate Invoice
1. Click **+ Create Invoice**
2. Select customer
3. Select order (auto-fills items)
4. Review totals
5. Click **Generate PDF**
6. Print or email to customer

---

## Step 5: View Reports

Click **REPORTS** tab.

### Available Reports
- **Sales Report**: Revenue by date/product
- **Inventory Report**: Stock levels over time
- **Customer Report**: Top customers, repeat buys
- **Stock Movement**: What's moving fast

**Tip:** Export to Excel for further analysis

---

## Step 6: Settings

Click **⚙️ Settings** (top right).

### Key Configurations
- **Company Details**: Logo, name, address
- **Pricing**: Currency, tax rate
- **Users**: Invite team members
- **Integrations**: Connect suppliers/email

---

✅ Check new orders → Pack → Mark shipped

**Weekly Checklist:**
✅ Run sales report

---

## Need Help?

- **Stuck on a step?** Hover over field labels for hints
- **Generate a report?** Reports tab → Export to Excel
- **Add a new user?** Settings → Users → Invite
