# Car Parts App - Quick Start Guide for Shippers

## Welcome! 👋

Your job: Pick orders from the warehouse and prepare them for shipment.

---

## Step 1: Login

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Click **Login**
3. You'll go directly to **ORDERS** tab

---

## Step 2: Your Daily Workflow

### View Pending Orders
1. Click **ORDERS** tab
2. You'll see "Ready to Pick" orders
3. Orders are listed by order date (oldest first)

### Pick an Order
1. Click on an order
2. **Check items** listed (part name, quantity, location)
3. Go to warehouse shelf location
4. **Scan barcode** or manually collect items
5. **Mark as Picked** in the app

### Review Picked Items
1. App shows checklist of items
2. **Tick off** each item as you collect
3. **Take a photo** (optional, for QA)
4. Click **Confirm Pick**

---

## Step 3: Pack & Label

### Pack the Order
1. Click the order
2. Click **Mark as Packed**
3. The system generates a **packing slip** (shows order number, items, customer address)
4. Print it and place in box

### Barcode Scan (Optional)
- Scan the order barcode on the slip to confirm
- Helps prevent mix-ups

---

## Step 4: Hand Off to Shipping

### Mark as Ready to Ship
1. Click order → **Ready to Ship**
2. Box is now in shipping area
3. Shipping carrier will pick it up

### Track It
1. Courier company will scan it
2. Tracking number auto-updates in system
3. Customer gets notified

---

## Common Issues

**"Item not in expected location?"**
- Check the warehouse map (ask manager)
- Use search to find item

**"Customer ordered wrong quantity?"**
- Mark it and take a photo
- Manager will review before shipping

**"Item damaged?"**
- Don't pack it
- Mark it as "Damaged" and note why
- Manager will resolve

---

## Your Dashboard

On login, you see:
- **# Orders Ready to Pick** (your queue)
- **# Orders Picked** (today)
- **# Packed** (ready to ship)

**Goal:** Clear your queue before end of shift ✅

---

## Quick Tips

✅ **Oldest orders first** — pick the first order in the list  
✅ **Check quantities** — don't eyeball it, count!  
✅ **Scan barcodes** when possible — reduces errors  
✅ **Take photos** of any issues for the manager  
✅ **Ask for help** if you can't find an item

---

## Need Help?

- **Can't find an item?** Ask the manager or check warehouse map
- **System not working?** Refresh the page
- **Questions?** Ask your supervisor
