# Car Parts App - Quick Start Guide for Stocktakers

## Welcome! 👋

Your job: Count and verify physical inventory against the system.

---

## Step 1: Login

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Click **Login**
3. You'll go directly to **STOCK TAKE** tab

---

## Step 2: Start a Stock Take

### Begin New Count
1. Click **STOCK TAKE** tab
2. Click **+ Start New Stock Take**
3. Choose the **location/warehouse** to count
4. System creates a new session
5. Click **Start Counting**

### You'll See
- List of parts that should be in this location
- Expected quantity from system
- **Quantity counted** (empty, for you to fill)
- Barcode scanner option

---

## Step 3: Count Your Inventory

### Method 1: Scan Barcodes (Recommended)
1. Use your barcode scanner
2. Scan each part's barcode
3. Enter the quantity you count
4. Click **Record**
5. Move to next item

### Method 2: Manual Count
1. Find a part on the list
2. Physically count items in warehouse
3. Enter quantity in the app
4. Click **Record**
5. Move to next item

### Tips for Accurate Count
✅ Count in zones (left to right, top to bottom)  
✅ Check high shelves and back corners  
✅ Don't rely on memory — count everything  
✅ Take photos if quantity looks wrong  

---

## Step 4: Handle Discrepancies

**If quantity doesn't match:**
- **System says 10, you count 8**: Enter 8. The system will flag the difference.
- **Find extra items**: Enter the count anyway. Manager will investigate.
- **Can't find an item**: Enter 0. Manager will update system.

---

## Step 5: Complete Stock Take

### Finish Counting
1. Go through entire list
2. Every item should have a "Counted" quantity
3. Click **Complete Stock Take**
4. Review summary (differences highlighted)
5. Click **Submit**

### Review Report
- System shows discrepancies
- Parts with quantity mismatches
- Photos you took (if any)

---

## Step 6: Follow-Up

**After Submission:**
- Manager reviews discrepancies
- Adjusts system if needed
- Contacts you if questions

**Your job:** Once submitted, you're done!

---

## Common Scenarios

**Item appears twice in different places?**
- Count it where you find it (one location only)
- Manager will consolidate if needed

**Item is damaged/unusable?**
- Still count it
- Write a note (optional)
- Manager will mark as "damaged"

**New items in stock not on list?**
- Still count them
- Note the part number
- Manager will add to system

---

## Your Dashboard

On login, you see:
- **Active Stock Takes**
- **Recent Counts** (this week)
- **Accuracy %** (your historical accuracy)

**Goal:** 100% accuracy ✅

---

## Quick Tips

✅ **Slow down** — rushing causes errors  
✅ **Use a checklist** — check off each section  
✅ **Take photos** of anything unusual  
✅ **Ask manager** if you can't identify a part  
✅ **Count twice** if you're unsure

---

## Need Help?

- **Can't identify a part?** Show photo to manager
- **Barcode scanner broken?** Count manually
- **System slow?** Try refreshing
- **Questions?** Ask your supervisor
