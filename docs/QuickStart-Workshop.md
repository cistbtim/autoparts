# Car Parts App - Quick Start Guide for Workshop Users

## Welcome! 👋

This guide covers the workshop module for vehicle servicing and job management.

---

## Step 1: Login

1. Enter your **email** and **password**
2. Click **Login**
3. You'll go directly to **WORKSHOP** tab

---

## Step 2: Mechanics — Your Workflow

### Book In a Vehicle

1. Click **WORKSHOP** tab
2. Click **+ Book New Job**
3. Enter customer details (name, phone)
4. Enter vehicle (make, model, year, registration)
5. Describe the work needed (e.g., "Engine oil change", "Brake pads replacement")
6. Click **Create Job**

**System assigns a job number** — show this to customer

### Record Work Done

1. Click on the job
2. Scroll to **Tasks** section
3. Click **+ Add Task** (or select from templates)
4. Enter time spent and any parts used
5. Click **Complete Task**

### Parts Used

1. In the job, click **Add Parts**
2. Search for part name/number
3. Enter quantity
4. System auto-calculates cost
5. Click **Add to Job**

### Generate Invoice

1. Job complete? Click **Generate Invoice**
2. Review costs (labour + parts)
3. Click **Print** or **Email to Customer**

---

## Step 3: Managers — Oversight & Quotes

### Monitor Jobs

1. Click **WORKSHOP** tab
2. See all active jobs
3. View status (In Progress / Waiting for Parts / Completed)
4. Click on job for details

### Create a Quote

**Before starting work:**
1. Click **+ Quote**
2. Enter customer + vehicle
3. Add parts needed
4. Add estimated labour hours
5. Click **Send Quote** (auto-calculates total)
6. Customer sees quote (via email/link)

### Approve/Manage Finances

1. Click **Invoicing**
2. Review invoices ready to send
3. Process payments
4. Generate reports

### Stock Management for Workshop

1. Click **Workshop Stock**
2. View parts in workshop inventory
3. Can order from main warehouse
4. Track usage

---

## Step 4: Request Supplier Quotes

### When You Need a Part

1. In the job, click **Request Quote from Supplier**
2. Enter part number + quantity
3. Select suppliers
4. System emails suppliers
5. Track responses here

### Review Supplier Quotes

1. Click **Supplier Quotes**
2. Compare pricing
3. Click **Accept** on best option
4. Part will be delivered to workshop

## Tips & Shortcuts

✅ **Job templates**: Save common jobs (e.g., "Oil Change", "Tyre Rotation")  
✅ **Time tracking**: Click timer button to auto-track hours  
✅ **Vehicle history**: Previous jobs shown when booking vehicle  
✅ **Parts history**: Quick-add parts used on similar vehicles  
✅ **Customer notes**: Add internal notes (not shown on invoice)

---

## Troubleshooting

**Part not in system?**
- Ask manager to add it (takes 1 min)
- Use "Custom Part" as workaround

**Customer disputed invoice amount?**
- Review job details
- Check parts used
- Contact manager

**Supplier not responding?**
- Try alternate supplier
- Contact supplier directly
- Document delay

---

## Dashboard

On login, you see:
- **Jobs In Progress** (your queue)
- **Today's Revenue** (parts + labour)
- **Pending Quotes** (awaiting supplier response)
- **Customer Messages** (new requests)
